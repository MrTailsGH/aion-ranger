1.3 Stagger - Bar
Some boss monsters have a stagger bar located directly beneath their health bar. This bar sometimes needs to be destroyed very quickly, otherwise the group will be attacked.
For example: Transcendenz 1 – Atiel
Every class has Stagger Values on certain skills. If you know a boss needs to be staggered, save these skills for when the bar opens. Arrow Storm provides the highest stagger value of all Ranger skills. Arrow Storm is a stigma skill.

3.1 Skill Chains
Once you have all the skills, build those skill chains. The macro is designed to provide the best damage output, but be aware that macros can change frequently due to balance patches and skill changes, so expect this one to be updated regularly. This macro was built on TW, so with a lower ping and adjustments from the global version, it's likely that a better one will be found in the future.
Marking Shot, Scattershot, Suppressing, Trap and Mother Nature are not part of the macro.
The reason for this is that: Marking Shot does not need to be spammed. It is a waste of animations, and it currently causes a lot of grief in terms of forced jump animations, which can lead to death. It's better to manually track the buff you gain from it and only use it manually to keep it up.
Mother Nature is a survival skill and would not be active at the correct time if used inside a macro. Suppressing isn't used at all and Trap is situational for AoE/grouping.

3.2 In Game Macro

Set up the macro like this: copy and paste the skill chains after registering a key for the macro in the game settings. For example, I use the right mouse button as my macro key.
To find the macro key settings, browse through the settings as follows:
Key Settings > General > Gameplay > Macro.
Choose a key that you like.

3.3 Razer

Now that you have set up the in-game macro and prepared your skill chains, we can move on to the final part: combining it with macro software. I personally use Razer, but if you are using different software, you will need to figure out how to set it up correctly yourself. It's important that you set the macro to run while the button is held down and to stop once released.

Frequently asked Questions about Macro:
Can I use macros from Day 1 ?
You can, but bear in mind that macros are always designed for the endgame, not the early game or story mode. You may experience clunky behaviour and rotas breaking until you reach certain checkpoints, especially with regard to combat speed, cooldown reduction and skill traits. There is no way around this due to the way progression and combat work in Aion 2. I tested the macro already on the network test for global after reaching level 16, and it worked for me even at a low level.
Why do we need a 3rd party? Can´t we just use the In-Game macro as a standalone?
In theory, you can only use the in-game macro, but this won't use stage 3 Deadshot, which results in significant damage loss. If you want to play without a third party to compensate, you will need to manually charge Deadshot towards stage three while holding LMB and the in-game macro.
Is this bannable?
No, it isn't. As long as you aren't building a macro that can run automatically (i.e. botting), you can use this tool without any problems.
Isn't it sup-optimal to run Buffs inside the macro?
Not really, since we usually run from boss to boss. It's also no issue to remove buffs from the macro and use them manually to have more control over them. Just try it out and see what you think. It's also good to know that thanks to the -1 sec CD Trait from Vaizels, you will later be able to keep it active with 100% uptime at certain combat speeds and CDR values. The same applies to Supporting Fire. The same applies to the Bow of Blessing, although it will take a bit longer since it has a longer base cooldown.
How to use the macro for sustain open world mob farming?
If you use the in-game macro key instead of the Razer key, you will automatically not use the Deadshot and LMB keybinds, which will make farming faster. If you are struggling with MP, you can either start holding the in-game macro key and the left mouse button (LMB) together, or use a second Razer macro without the Deadshot keybind.
Macro isn´t working for me?
If the macro doesn't work correctly, you've probably done something wrong. Feel free to ask for help in #Ranger-General, but bear in mind that neither I nor anyone else there is your technical support, especially if you don't use Razer as your macro software.

Chapter 4: Daevanion Board
Priority: Orange > Blue > Green > White, on all boards. The board is a marathon, not a sprint, the rules below keep every point you spend efficient.
1. Start with the top-priority skills and passives (see Chapter 1) and work your way through them until you have unlocked most of them.

2. For white tiles: Attack > Critical Hit > HP > Defence > Critical Defence > MP.

3. Sometimes it’s better to prioritise blue over orange if you can reach a skill milestone for active skills (levels 8, 12, 16 and 20).

4. If you cannot max out a board, try not to waste points on anything that does not increase your offensive attributes. Defence is considered less important.

5. You can always reset the boards using the main in-game currency, Kinah.

All Boards after the first 4 can be fully maxed and therefore do not appear in this guide.

Chapter 5: Pets
Four types: Cogni, Fera, Natura, Varian. Pets give a lot of flat stats and are important.
Level priority: Cogni > Fera > Natura > Varian
5.1 Global launch
Focus on equipment rather than pets at launch. Pets will come over time, and you can later also get them from the daily dungeon. Don’t invest too much in this early on, gear will carry you further in the first few weeks. Once you have downtime between content you can start farming Pets.
5.2 Genus Insight System - What to aim for on Slot 1-9
General rules for rolling Slots:

1. First, aim for slot 4, after slot 7. After that, you can decide whether or not you want more HP. If not, you can skip the HP% and aim directly for the other Atk and Acc/Crit on the other slots.
2. Max Attack is better than Attack since it scales of Weapon Damage Boost
3. Do not lock slots until level 10, except slots 4/7 - if you get a yellow/orange role with around 80% or higher
4. On your first preset, keeping rolls with around 75-80% or more is fine, going for 90-95% or more is a very long process and you will burn through thousands of pet crystals, therefore you should aim for those min maxed values in your second preset NOT your first.
5. Only use Rainbow Crystals for Special Genus Insight.

Chapter 6: Gear and Stats

I don’t recommend min-maxing anything except PvE crafted gear. I am not to be blamed for materials or money wasted on gear that isn’t worth it; if you’re not sure whether to upgrade something, ask first. Crafted gear is not considered to not be cheap but it is the best in slot Gear, so keep that in mind.

This guide focuses on the best PvE-Rolls for every Gear piece to reach the best end result in terms of DPS. For every Piece of gear there will be a priority order since different tiers of gear ( Yellow, Red, etc ) have different amounts of lines for roles. As for what gear to aim for, PvE Crafted Gear is considered to be the best gear. So you will focus on building this as soon as possible to transition out of dungeon gear.
Important: If you are a F2P player or a sub only you most likely will not be able to afford the crafted armor in season 1. You will not lose out on a lot of damage by using dungeon armor and it is common in Taiwan and Korea as well. However if you are a light spender or want to min max you definitely will go with full crafted gear regardless.

6.2 Base Stats
Stats are divided into 3 Categories. Divine Stats, Main Stats and Sup Stats.
Your main focus will be Attack, Crit and Accuracy for Main Stats as well as Might and Precision.
Also you will heavily focus into sup stats:
Double Chance ( gives double DMG if triggered )
Perfect Chance ( your weapon has min and max rng dmg , perfect makes it always hit with highest possible dmg role )
Combat Speed ( makes you attack faster )
Cooldown Reduction ( lets you use more skills )
Double Chance > Perfect Chance in terms of focus.
CDR and Combat Speed mostly come from Divine Stats and Equipment Soulbinds.

Chapter 7: Arcanas
Stats on arcanas are random and need a lot of luck, so even a top player isn’t always running perfect arcanas. Through transmute preview you can always see which skills you can get at which arcana.
Every Arcana Card can be upgraded from 0 up until +5. The higher the enhancement level is, the more expensive it becomes. With every enhancement lvl a random stat/skill line will be upgraded.
Therefore my recommendation is to not +5 every Arcana, instead just to +1 or +2 and check if the stats you want to go up are going up, if not try again on a different arcana.

Chapter 8: Pantheon
In terms of the Pantheon you have 3 different types of things you can place.

1. Artworks
2. Statues
3. Colossus

Depending on the rarity you will have different amounts of stat lines. Hence why you aim for Yellow/Orange long term.

You aim for Artworks that have Illusion + Wisdom. As a third stat there most of the time will be Destiny or Life. Life is better. But at the start of the game there won't be enough Artworks with Wisdom,Illusion, Life so you will use Wisdom,Illusion, Destiny as a filler.

On Statues you want to aim for Yellow ones with Freedom, Death and Space. Early on you will use blue as filler with only 2 lines here. Go for either Death + Freedom or Death + Space, up to you I would recommend Freedom + Space, eventually less damage early on but the movement speed from Space helps a lot with evading and playing mechanics early on.

As for the Colossus in Season 1 you will use Zikel/Kromede and with the Start of Season 2 you will want to switch as soon as possible towards Kaisinel. Everything else is considered no option.
